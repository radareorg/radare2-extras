# uSIP (MD32 family, VLIW packet variant) — ISA table notes for the `usip` CPU mode

Reference for the table format, the constraint semantics and the packet model behind the `usip` CPU mode.  Everything
in it is implemented by `usip_decode.c` (packet model), `tools/gen_usip_tables.py` (table) and `arch_md32.c` (RAnalOp
mapping); see README.md "uSIP mode" for the user-facing description.  Files:

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `isa/usip_isa.json` | the machine-readable table: `meta`, `structure` (packet model, registers, CSRs, system ops, I/O ports, conditions, displacement and immediate formulas, field types, context rules) and the ORDERED list `forms` |
| `usip_opcodes.h` | the same forms as a C table (`UsipOpcode usip_opcodes[]`), generated by `tools/gen_usip_tables.py` |
| `usip_decode.c` | the packet decoder (`parse_group`, `group_parses`, `score`, `parse_packet`) |

## 1. The packet model

The earlier `usip_decode()` read one 16-bit halfword and, when bit 15 was 0, paired it with the next halfword. That is
wrong in general: **the unit is the 16-bit halfword and the instruction stream is a VLIW packet stream.**

1. **Unit scan.** Read halfwords (big-endian) from the packet start. A packet = consecutive units up to and
   including the first unit with **bit 15 = 1** (max 8 units). Packets start at `addr % 4 == 2` as often as at
   `addr % 4 == 0`: never assume 32-bit words. Branch targets are packet starts.
2. **Heads and tails.** Inside a packet, a unit with bit 15 = 0 is either a *head* (first unit of an instruction) or
   a *tail* (second/third unit of a 32/48-bit instruction). Instructions are **head + 0, 1 or 2 tails**, 16/32/48 bits.
   A head without a tail is a 16-bit op "with the packet still open" (its bit 15 is 0 but it is decoded like the
   bit-15 = 1 form; most 16-bit entries of the table mask bit 15 out, the stand-alone *closer* forms keep it).
3. **Layout (head/tail pairing).** Tails are not necessarily adjacent to their head: the packet is *heads first,
   then tails*, and the tails are dealt **round-robin** among the heads that take tails, in head order (`S S O O` =
   two 32-bit instructions interleaved; `x008 X hi hi' lo lo'` = a 48-bit absolute call interleaved with another
   48-bit op). A packet may also be a sequence of *groups*, each laid out that way (one-instruction groups =
   contiguous layout: `b dest ‖ delay-slot ops`). No bit selects the layout. The decoder enumerates every group
   split and every head/tail assignment allowed by the table (a head may take a tail iff some form matches
   head+tail; a head may stand alone unless it is in `structure.packet.must_pair` — `op C` with F = 0, `6008`,
   `st.abs`, `?cX0` — and `ret/save/restore` never take tails), discards, inside a group, the assignments with more
   bare heads that have no named 16-bit reading, and keeps the reading with the fewest *unknown units*, then the
   fewest instructions, then the fewest groups, then the documented tie-break penalties
   (`structure.packet.tie_breaks`). Two parser rules complete the enumeration: **R0** — the final closer (bit 15 = 1)
   may be a one-unit group of its own even when it has no named 16-bit reading (layout H T C); **R7** — a bare
   first-slot unit with bit 15 = 0 of the slot family `[0][1][0][h][rrr][x][B][o3 o2 00]` (or of the `lea?` shape
   `[0][0][0][h][rrr][x][B][o 000]` with rA ≠ r14 when no other instruction of the packet has a tail) counts as a named
   unit when every other instruction of the packet is named, and is printed through the context forms
   `ldslot_S0`/`stslot_S0`/`lea_sp_S0`. The 3-unit candidate `[head][op16][F' = 0 tail]` falls out of the round-robin
   enumeration. The +2 guard that nulls a set-on-compare pair whose tail is not adjacent applies only to the
   immediate forms (`USIP_F_ADJ1`), never to the op 7 register forms. Equal scores keep the first enumerated
   assignment: the assignments where the latest heads take the tails are enumerated first. Most packets have a
   single consistent reading; a greedy fallback (pair each head with the next free unit if some form matches,
   round-robin) is used when no group parse exists.
4. **Slot 0.** Only the first unit of a packet can be control flow (branches, `call/b`, `loop`, 16-bit branches incl.
   `call.s` with bit 15 = 0, `beqz.d/bnez.d` with F = S = 0, `call.abs/b.abs`); forms flagged `USIP_F_SLOT0` must not
   be matched elsewhere (the same bits in other slots are `mov/add/op C...`). The 32-bit `ret` (`40ff c000`), `ret?`,
   `jr? rB` and `call rB` of the `sys` shape are NOT slot-0 forms. The tail of a branch/call/loop must be at +1 (tail
   with S = 1) or at most +2 (S = 0); the control tails with **F' = 0** (`call_F0`/`b_F0` = `[S][0][1L]`, `bcc_F0` =
   `[1][0][tt]`, flag `USIP_F_ADJ2`) sit **exactly at +2** — the unit in between is an independent 16-bit op (the
   delay slot); at +1 or +3 the pair is data (`.word`). `loop_F0` keeps its exception (tail at +2 even with S = 1).
   **Target guards.** Several forms carry `cons = guard:target_*`: `target_packet_start_same_function` (`loop_F0`,
   `bcc_F0`, `beqz_d`/`bnez_d`), `target_function_start` (`call_F0`, `call_s_S0`), `target_fn_or_packet_start_same_function`
   (`b_far`, `b_F0`). For 32-bit forms the guard is evaluated **after** the mask match and a failure makes the pair
   `.word` (no fall-through to the next entry); for the 16-bit `call_s_S0` a failure falls through to the next 16-bit
   entry. Section 6 describes what the plugin can answer.
5. **Delay slot = the rest of the packet.** When the control instruction does not close the packet (`call`/`b` with
   an S = 0 tail, `b<cond>.d`, `beqz.d`/`bnez.d`, `ret.d` = `5ef9`, `b.s` with bit 15 = 0, `call.s` with bit 15 = 0,
   `call rB`, `call.abs`, `jr?`), every other unit of the packet executes before the transfer (on both paths). For the
   F' = 0 tails at +2 the unit between head and tail is the delay slot (and the rest of the packet when the tail has
   S = 0). It never reaches the next packet. For radare2 this means `op->delay` = number of remaining instructions in
   the packet, and the branch instruction's `size` covers only its own units while the fall-through address of the
   packet is the next packet.
6. **Hardware loops.** `loop rX, last=pc+disp12`: `last` is the address of the LAST instruction of the body
   (inclusive); the body is the following 1-16 instructions.

Operand fields are given as bit ranges over the big-endian concatenation of the instruction's units (bit `width-1`
= bit 15 of the head): 32-bit forms = head bits 31..16, tail 15..0; 48-bit = head 47..32, hi 31..16, lo 15..0.
When the units are not adjacent in memory the plugin gathers them (head, then its tails in round-robin order)
before applying mask/value.

## 2. The table

`tools/gen_usip_tables.py` emits every field referenced by the format string or by a constraint (`nfields` >=
`nargs`), the constraints as structured `UsipCons` records (op, field indices, values), the indices of the
context-only forms (`USIP_FORM_*`) and the auxiliary tables of the ISA description (`usip_must_pair`,
`usip_sys_fld4[16]`, `usip_csr_ext[64]`, `usip_kind_names[]`, `usip_save_set[8]`); the generated header is
`usip_opcodes.h` and regenerating it is byte-identical.

`usip_opcodes[]` is ordered: the first entry of the right width whose `(units & mask) == value`, whose `flags`
allow the slot, and whose `cons` holds is the reading. Fields of each entry: `name` (mnemonic), `form` (stable id =
`usip_isa.json` `forms[].name`), `value/mask`, `width/nunits`, `flags` (`USIP_F_SLOT0`, `NOTSLOT0`, `CTX`, `DELAY`,
`ADJ1`, `ADJCTRL`, `NAMED`, `NOTAIL`, `ADJ2`, `UNKNOWN16`), `kind`, `conf`, `fmt` + `fields[]` (operand template and
bit fields), `cons[]`.

Operand value transforms (`UsipField.type`): `reg` (`rN`; the `{N:!}` template token prints the high-bank `!`;
`base` holds a constant ADDED to the field — `rB1 = rB + 1` of `ld.d?/st.d?`, `sh = m + 1` of the 16-bit `lsl`), `imm`
(× `scale` when declared: `addsp`, `lea?`, `ld.d?` offsets), `simm` (sign-extend to the total field width, × `scale`),
`rotimm` (`rot(f,n)`: bit 0 is the sign, `sext((f>>1)|((f&1)<<(n-1)), n)`), `urot`, `disp` (signed, × `scale`,
pc-relative: target = head address + value), `udisp` (loop end, inclusive), `absaddr` (`base | value`, `base = SEG[A]
= A << 26`, `0x78000000` for the hi(1010) family), `acc` (`A(value & 3)`), `accw` (`mfacc` word: `A(w/3).{lo,hi,ext}`),
`csr` (classic bank name table `usip_csr_classic`; no form uses it), `csr_ext`, `flag`, `movhi` (the five collected
bits `(op[0], t[13], u, f12[9], f12[8])` → `(b4<<31)|(b3<<30)|(b2 ? 0x3fe00000 : 0)|(b1<<20)|(b0<<19)`).

Bank bits: `x` (head/unit bit 8) is the bank of **rB** (destination / data: `mov rB!, rA`, 32-bit ld/st data, the
second source of op 7 / op C, `ior/iow` data, the slot family), `m` (bit 13) is the bank of **rA** (source / base:
`mov rB, rA!`, `[rA!+off]`, `add rB, rA!, #imm`), and for op 7 (78-7b / 7c / 7d) `ss & 2` (head bit 13, field `ss1`)
puts **rs1** in the high bank. Templates print them as `{x:!}`, `{m:!}`, `{ss1:!}`. The 32-bit
`ld.w/ld.h/ld.hu/st.w/st.h` offset is **unsigned** (0..4095); `ld.b` keeps `sext12` (field `0x800` prints `+1`,
formula open); `st.b?` uses `sext11` with bit 11 as a flag.

Constraints that are not mask/value (`cons` records, evaluated by `cons_ok()`): a few address windows on the 48-bit
absolute forms (`call.abs`, `mov32`, `ld.w [#imm27]`), `B != 0` for `sys`, `rA not in {14,15}` for the 16-bit
`bz.s/bnz.s`, the near-`b` displacement range, three *guards* that look at whether the tail unit would be a named
16-bit op (`setcc_tail`, `tail_not_named16` — also on `st_b_q`, `st_w_op1_F1S0`, `mtcsr_q` —, `or2_tail`), `field >= n`
(`ge`: `addsp_S0` |imm7| ≥ 17 as `(imm7 < -16 || imm7 >= 17)`), `field in [..]` on hidden fields (`u9` uniform middle
bits of `movhi?`, `rA5` ≠ 14 of `bcc_F0`, `f12 in {2,4,6}` of `.op8.idx?`, the `csr` number sets of `mfcsr/mtcsr`, stored
as 64-bit membership masks), `packet_len == 1` (= stand-alone unit: `ldslot/stslot`, `lea?`, `movi #simm6`, the class-E
h = 1 and `.bit4?/.ext8?` shapes; evaluated with the real packet length, unknown = fails, so those forms never count
as named 16-bit ops for the pairing predicates), 16-bit entries whose **mask includes bit 15 = 1** (match only packet
closers: `addsp`, `.slot?`, `lsl`, `movi?`, `movi rA, #-1`), and the target guards of §1.4 (records
`USIP_C_GUARD_TARGET_*`, evaluated after the match with the knowledge of §6). `kind` has `io` (`ior/iow`) and `jr`
(`jr? rB`, treated like `ret` for the delay slot).

Context rules (not table entries, see `usip_isa.json structure.context_rules`): `.lo` after a loose `hi(1010)`
syllable, `.ext` (`fX?7` after a `ld/st` whose tail has S = 0), `.be79` (bare `[0][0][imm14]` + `be79`), the
2-unit-packet `bz.s` (`USIP_F_CTX` entry `bz_s_P4`, rA ≠ r14), and the first-slot forms `ldslot_S0` / `stslot_S0` /
`lea_sp_S0` (`USIP_F_CTX`, rule R7 of §1.3). `structure.sys_ops` lists the `sys`-shape families by tail field (fld 0
`ret`/`jr?`, 1 `call rB`, 2 `ret?`, 4 `cop/rti/lpexit?/isync?/ieon/ieoff/trap?/sys? #13/idle`),
`structure.registers.io_ports` the port map of `ior/iow`.

## 3. Confidence and what is format-only

* `high`: encoding and operation established by independent checks — the whole control flow (`b<cond>`, `call`,
  16-bit branches, `loop`, `call.abs`, the F' = 0 tails), ld/st with offset, the 16-bit ALU, the 32-bit ALU
  (`op 7 + 78-7b`), `movi21`, `mov32`, the basop/DSP family (`op C`), mul/div, bit ops, port I/O, `save/restore`,
  `nop32`, `cmov r15`, `sne`, `addsp`.
* `medium`: statistically established or with one confirmed example, or sign/width open (`ld.hu?`, `ld.bu?`, `st.h?`,
  `ld.w?` stack slots, far `b`, SP adjustments, `cmov` bank bit, `rd!` high-bank twins, `ld.d?/st.d?` (64-bit pair
  width), `st.b?`, `lea?`, `ldslot/stslot` (slot base open), `movhi?` (bit order), `scmp?` (relation), `sne?` (other
  immediates), `mtcsr` (`mtcsr?` for 0x30-0x3e/0x10-0x14), `mfcsr?`, `beqz.d/bnez.d`, `ret32`, `ret?`, `jr?`,
  `lpexit?`, `isync?`, `trap?`, `extu`, `ior?/iow?` (x = 1), the 16-bit `ld.w/st.w +12/+20` cells and `lsl #1/#2`,
  `movi #simm6`).
* `low`: named hypotheses (`clr.a?`, `msu?`, `.osl?`, `.cmp?`, `or.2`, `add.1`, accumulator shifts, `sys?`,
  `ldslot?/stslot?`).
* `format-only`: the pairing is established (which units belong together) but not the operation: mnemonics starting
  with `.` (`.hword`, `.word`, `.dsp`, `.alu32`, `.op4imm/.op8?/.opd?`, `.op7t`, `.bf?`, `.idx?`, `.ctl15`, `.1eX0`,
  `.6008`, `.ld.abs`, `.hi/.lo/.hilo/.ext`, `.fill`, `.st7`, `.slot?`, `.op5i?`, `.sgn?`...). The plugin still groups
  their units (so that the packet/delay-slot model stays right) and prints them as data.

Open points that matter for a disassembler: the second register bank (`!`) size; bit 13 (`m`) of the 32-bit forms
and bit 8 (`x`) outside the places where `!` is validated; the exact `save w` frame size; the sign of the half/byte
loads of the 48-bit family; `sys #2/#4/#8`; `L_mult` vs `L_mult.e` (two encodings, one operation); the base of the
`ldslot/stslot` slots.

## 4. ABI (for `regs()`)

`r14 = SP`, `r15 = LR`, arguments `r0-r7` (r0-r3 most used, stack args > r7), return `r0` (`r0:r1` for 64 bits;
64-bit products live in the accumulators `A0-A3`), callee-saved `r8-r10, r12, r13` (+ the high-bank copies) via
`save w` (`w = 2` saves `{r11!,r12!,r13!,r14!}`: four words), `r11` caller-saved. There is **no classic CSR bank**
reachable through op 4: that shape is port I/O (`ior rB, [rA]` / `iow rB, [rA]`, rA = port number,
`structure.registers.io_ports`); the MD32 names `sr ipc isr …` are kept only for the `csr` field type of the md32
plugin. Extended CSRs `0x01-0x3e` via `mfcsr rB, #nn` (tail F' free) and `mtcsr #nn, rA`: `0x20 lf`, `0x21-0x29
lc/ls/le 0-2`, `0x30 + 4k + part` = `Ak.{lo,hi,ext}`. There are no flags: all branches are compare-and-branch.

## 5. Known limitations of the table-driven reading

1. **Ties.** In `S S O O` and longer packets several head/tail assignments can have the same number of unknown
   units and instructions (`mov || X` vs `st.h rB,[rA+off]`, `inc || X` vs `add rB, rA, #imm`, which head takes a
   `ld.h` tail...). The decoder keeps the first enumerated reading and sets `opex.packet.ambiguous`.
2. **Control flow in open packets** (`call`/`b`/`loop`/`b.s` followed by delay-slot ops): when the branch reading
   is not the cheapest one the packet reads as `add`/`st.w`/`movi`. A disassembler that knows the function table
   can prefer the reading whose target is a function start or a packet boundary.
3. **Target guards that cannot be evaluated** (§6): far `b` to another function, the open-packet `call.s`, and
   `loop`/`bnez.d`/register `b<cond>` with the F' = 0 tail and a forward target beyond the lookahead are read as data
   or as the plain 16-bit units.

## 6. Target guards: what the plugin knows

The decoder asks the caller through `UsipGuardCtx` (`packet_start`, `function_start`: yes / no / unknown).
`arch_md32.c` answers **packet starts from the stream**: the packet starts decoded earlier in the session (a hash set
in the session state: backward targets, and forward targets of a region decoded before, e.g. after `aa` or on a second
listing) and a scan of the bytes radare2 passes with the op past the current packet (forward targets; radare2 caps the
op bytes at 64, `r_anal_op_set_bytes`), so a forward target farther than 64 bytes is unknown on a first linear pass.
"Same function" is approximated by "reachable in the linear decode" (no function bounds). **Function starts are
unknown**: an arch plugin has no typed access to RAnal (`RArch.user` is an untyped pointer), so no callback is
installed; `UsipGuardCtx.function_start` is the hook for an embedder that has a function table.

When a guard cannot be evaluated the decoder applies a fallback chosen by measurement on the sample the table was
built from (`unverified_ok()` in `usip_decode.c`): the forms whose unverified readings are right in nearly every case
where they compete are read anyway — `call_F0` (function start unknown; with the tie-break bonus), `b_F0`,
`beqz_d` and the immediate `b<cond>` forms with the F' = 0 tail (`beq/bne/ble/blt/bgtu/bge/bgt_imm8_F0`, forward
target beyond the lookahead); the others are treated as a failed guard — `call_s_S0` (only a small fraction of the
shapes are calls), `b_far` (about a fifth of the unverified readings would be wrong), `bnez_d` (the cell is shared
with the op C DSP pairs), `ble_rr_F0`/`blt_rr_F0` (shared with `.ctl15`), `loop_F0` (`movi || mov` pairs). An
accepted-unverified reading prints a trailing `?` (`call?`, `beqz.d?`, `blt?`), reports `confidence = medium` and
`target_guard = "unverified"` in `opex`; a validated one reports `"validated"`.
